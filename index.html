Halftab is a calm dose tracker for ADHD medication. Log half a tab in two taps, check in on how you feel, watch each dose peak and fade, and see what's left, today and in the bottle.

Coming soon to the App Store

No account. No ads. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Split tabs

Quarter, half, three-quarters, or one and a half. The milligrams follow, so your daily total is always right.

See what's active

Every dose glows along a sun arc that follows the day, or, with Plus, as a ring on a 24-hour clock. Tap any dose to see when it begins to wear off.

How are you feeling right now?

Check in any time. Quick picks for tired, jittery, focused, anxious, calm, and headache, or the full list by category. Each one is time-stamped against your doses.

Insights that grow with you

Every check-in lands on the dose curve. Halftab finds patterns in your own logs: when jittery hits, whether tired comes before the estimated wear-off, late doses and sleep, half tabs versus full, caffeine days. Each card says how much data it rests on.

A report for your doctor

A one-page monthly PDF of prescriptions, doses, how the month felt, patterns worth discussing, notes, and pickups. Share it before an appointment.

Never run out

Log each pharmacy pickup. Doses count down what's on hand, and a week before the month is up, Halftab flags the day to request a refill.

Estimated timing, tuned to you

Optional estimates for when a dose kicks in, wears off, and clears for sleep. Tell Halftab if you metabolize fast or slow, dose with a meal, or eat a lot of acidic food, and they shift to match.

Every ADHD medication

38 products with every strength and illustrated pill artwork, from Adderall and Vyvanse to Concerta and Strattera. Anything else, add your own.
