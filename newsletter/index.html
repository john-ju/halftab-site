Halftab by email

One email when Halftab is on the App Store, then a new guide a few times a year.

You'll get a short welcome email right away. Unsubscribe anytime from any email.

What you'll get

  • The launch email. One message the day Halftab is on the App Store.
  • New guides. Each guide as it's published: plain-language answers about ADHD medication timing and dosing, checked against FDA prescribing information. A few a year, not a schedule.
  • What's new in the app, when there's something worth a note.

What we won't do

  • Share or sell your address. It's used for these emails and nothing else.
  • Name a medication in a subject line. Subjects say "Halftab" and a topic, so a lock-screen preview or a shared inbox doesn't say more than you'd want it to.
  • Tie it to the app. Halftab has no accounts and never asks for your email. This list lives on the website, and what you log in the app stays on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • Make leaving hard. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one tap removes you.

Emails are sent through Kit, which stores your address on our behalf. See the privacy policy for the details.
