Halftab is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not identify pills. Nothing in the app is a substitute for the judgment of your prescribing clinician or pharmacist. Take medication only as prescribed. If you have questions about your medication, dosing, or side effects, contact your clinician. If you believe you have taken too much of a medication, contact emergency services or your local poison control center immediately.

2. Estimates and accuracy

Onset, peak, wear-off, and sleep-time estimates are approximations derived from published release characteristics of medication types. Real effects vary widely by person, food, sleep, other medications, and many other factors. Totals are computed from the numbers you enter; if you enter the wrong strength or quantity, the totals will be wrong. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you log and for verifying anything you rely on.

Pill illustrations are artistic representations following general brand appearances and may not match the medication you have been dispensed. Never use them to identify a pill.
