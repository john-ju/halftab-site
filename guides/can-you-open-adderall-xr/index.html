Can you open or split Adderall XR?

You can open an Adderall XR capsule and sprinkle everything inside on applesauce. You can't split one into smaller doses. Here's the difference, what the label says, and what to do if you need less.

By the Halftab team · Checked against FDA prescribing information · Updated October 3, 2026

The short answer

  • Opening is fine. You can open the capsule and sprinkle all of the beads on applesauce, then swallow it right away without chewing.
  • Splitting is not. The label says the dose of a single capsule should not be divided. Don't take half the beads or save some for later.
  • Never chew or crush the beads. Some of them are coated to release later, and breaking the coating releases that dose early.

What's inside the capsule

An Adderall XR capsule holds two kinds of beads. One kind releases right away. The other has a coating that delays its release. The label says one capsule gives about the same levels as two doses of immediate-release Adderall taken four hours apart.

That's why you can't divide a capsule. If you pour out half the beads, you have no way of knowing how many of each kind you got. You might take mostly the early beads one day and mostly the late ones the next.

How to sprinkle Adderall XR on applesauce

  1. Put a spoonful of applesauce in a small dish or on a spoon.
  2. Open the capsule over it and empty out all of the beads.
  3. Swallow it right away without chewing.
  4. Don't save any. Throw away anything you didn't take, and don't prepare it ahead of time.

The label says a capsule sprinkled on applesauce is absorbed about the same as one swallowed whole. Opening it doesn't make it work faster or stronger.

If you need a smaller or shorter dose

  • Ask for a lower strength. Adderall XR comes in 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 mg capsules.
  • Ask about immediate-release tablets. Adderall IR tablets are scored and can be split when a prescriber orders it. See can you split Adderall.
  • Tell your prescriber what you're noticing. If it lasts too late into the evening or fades too early, they can change the strength or the form. See how long Adderall lasts for the usual timeline.
Taking part of a capsule to stretch a supply or lower your dose changes what you're taking in a way nobody can measure. Talk to your prescriber first, especially during shortages.

Generics and Mydayis

Generic Adderall XR is sold as mixed amphetamine salts extended-release capsules. It follows the same rules as the brand. Mydayis is a longer-acting capsule with three kinds of beads. Its label also allows opening the capsule and sprinkling the entire contents on applesauce, and it shouldn't be divided either.

Common questions

Can you split Adderall XR in half?

No. The Adderall XR label says the dose of a single capsule should not be divided. The capsule holds a mix of immediate and delayed beads, and there's no way to pour out an even half.

Can you open an Adderall XR capsule?

Yes. You can open the capsule and sprinkle the entire contents on applesauce. Swallow it right away without chewing, and don't store it for later.

Can you put Adderall XR in water or yogurt?

The label only describes sprinkling the beads on applesauce. Ask your pharmacist before using anything else.

Does opening Adderall XR make it kick in faster?

No. The label says a capsule sprinkled on applesauce is absorbed about the same as one swallowed whole. Chewing or crushing the beads is different. It breaks the delayed-release coating and shouldn't be done.

Can you chew the beads in Adderall XR?

No. Some of the beads are coated to release later in the day. Chewing or crushing them releases that part of the dose early.
