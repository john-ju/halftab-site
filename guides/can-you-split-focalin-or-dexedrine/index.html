Can you split Focalin or Dexedrine?

Scored dextroamphetamine tablets can be split when prescribed. Focalin tablets have no score line, and the long-acting capsules should never be divided. Here's what the labels say for Focalin, Focalin XR, dextroamphetamine tablets, and Dexedrine Spansules.

By the Halftab team · Checked against FDA prescribing information · Updated October 3, 2026

The short answer

  • Dextroamphetamine tablets (Zenzedi and generics) are often scored in the 5 mg and 10 mg strengths. Split one only if it has a score line and your prescriber has ordered a half dose.
  • Focalin tablets have no score line, in the brand or in the generics we checked. Ask about a lower strength instead. They start at 2.5 mg.
  • Focalin XR capsules can be opened and sprinkled on applesauce, but you take all of it. Don't divide the beads.
  • Dexedrine Spansule capsules are extended release. Swallow them whole.

Two different medications, one question

Focalin and Dexedrine aren't the same drug. Focalin is dexmethylphenidate, a close relative of Ritalin. Dexedrine is dextroamphetamine, one of the ingredients in Adderall. They're covered together here because the question is the same for both: do you have a long-acting capsule or an immediate-release tablet, and does that tablet have a score line?

Why the capsules can't be divided

Focalin XR holds two kinds of beads in equal amounts. Half release right away, and half are coated to release later. If you pour out part of a capsule, you can't know how many of each kind you got. Chewing or crushing the beads breaks the coating and releases the later dose early.

A Dexedrine Spansule works in a similar way. Part of the dose is released promptly and the rest gradually over the day. Opening or dividing it isn't something the label covers.

How to split a scored tablet cleanly

  1. Use a pill splitter, not a knife or your fingers.
  2. Line the blade up with the score and press straight down.
  3. Split close to when you'll take it, and keep the spare half in the original bottle.
  4. If a tablet crumbles, don't guess at the dose. Ask your pharmacist what to do.

If you need a smaller dose

  • Ask for a lower strength. Focalin tablets start at 2.5 mg and Focalin XR capsules at 5 mg. Dextroamphetamine tablets come in strengths as low as 2.5 mg.
  • Ask about the liquid. Dextroamphetamine oral solution lets a prescriber order doses a tablet can't give.
  • Keep count in halves. If you do split, log each dose in tablets and milligrams, like “½ tab, 5 mg,” so your daily total and your refill date stay right.
Splitting to take more or less than prescribed changes your dose. Talk to your prescriber first, especially during shortages. They may be able to switch strengths or forms instead.

Common questions

Can you cut Focalin in half?

Focalin tablets have no score line, and the label gives no instructions for splitting them. If you need a smaller dose, ask your prescriber about a lower strength. Tablets start at 2.5 mg. Focalin XR capsules should not be divided.

Can you open Focalin XR?

Yes. You can open a Focalin XR capsule and sprinkle the entire contents on applesauce. Swallow it without chewing. Don't crush, chew, or divide the beads.

Can you split Dexedrine?

Immediate-release dextroamphetamine tablets can be split if they are scored and a prescriber orders a half dose. Dexedrine Spansule capsules are extended release and should be swallowed whole.

Can you open a Dexedrine Spansule?

The Dexedrine Spansule label gives no instructions for opening the capsule. Swallow it whole, and ask your pharmacist if you have trouble swallowing capsules.

Are Focalin and Dexedrine the same thing?

No. Focalin is dexmethylphenidate, related to Ritalin. Dexedrine is dextroamphetamine, related to Adderall. They are different medications.
